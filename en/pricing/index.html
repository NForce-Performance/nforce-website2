Coaching packages

Three tracks, one method: measure, plan, execute, retest. Minimum term is twelve weeks — anything shorter gives no honest conclusion. Monthly prices include VAT, cancellable monthly after the first twelve weeks.

Base

€49 per month

For the athlete who trains independently and mainly lacks a solid plan.

  • Intake and test analysis
  • Monthly programme in the app
  • Video technique feedback, once a week
  • Retest after every six-week block

Frequently asked questions

Do monthly prices include VAT?

Yes, the monthly prices shown include VAT. Team rates exclude VAT.

Can I pay quarterly?

Yes, for Performance and Return-to-Play. Mention it at the intake.

Is there a student discount?

Players under eighteen and students with a valid card get ten percent off the monthly price.

Know where your gain is within 20 minutes

The Performance Check is free, takes twenty minutes and is not a sales call. You get an honest read on your biggest limiter and the advice that follows from it — even if that means you don't need to buy anything.
