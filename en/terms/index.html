Based on a written confirmation of assignment. Free rescheduling up to seven days before the test day; after that fifty percent is charged.

Health

I am not a doctor or physiotherapist. With complaints, pain or a medical condition, medical advice takes precedence over mine. If you train with an injury, I only cooperate when your practitioner supports it.

Participation is at your own risk. Liability is limited to the amount of the last invoice, except in cases of intent or gross negligence.
