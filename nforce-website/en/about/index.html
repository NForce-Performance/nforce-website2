About Nick

I work with numbers because feel lies too often

I'm Nick Bergman, performance coach in Tilburg. I work daily with ice hockey players and team-sport athletes on strength, speed, turning and robustness — on the floor and online.

My background is Fontys University of Applied Sciences for Sport; my practice is in the gym and on the ice. What I see there is almost always the same: players working hard on what they already do well, leaving the domain that holds them back untouched. That's not a motivation problem, it's an information problem.

So everything starts with measuring and ends with retesting. Not because numbers are everything, but because they keep the conversation honest. If a block doesn't work, I want to know after six weeks — not after a season.
